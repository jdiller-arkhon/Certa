# Getting the Certa app

Certa installs straight from your web browser. There is no app store and nothing to pay. You
end up with a Certa icon on your phone's Home Screen or your computer, and it opens in its own
window like any other app.

The same steps are built into Certa itself: open **Get the app** (`/certa/install`), or in the
app choose your name at the top right, then **Install the app**. That page picks your device
for you and, on a computer, shows a QR code you can scan with your phone.

> Replace `https://<your-certa-address>` below with the address your organization uses for
> Certa (for Arkhon: the `/certa` page on the Arkhon website, once it's live).

---

## iPhone and iPad

Use **Safari**. Chrome and Edge on iPhone also work, from their own Share button.

1. **Open Certa in Safari.** Go to `https://<your-certa-address>/install`.
2. **Tap Share.** It's the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on
   iPhone and at the top on iPad. If you don't see it, tap **•••** first, then **Share**.
3. **Tap "Add to Home Screen".** Scroll down the list if it isn't showing. Leave
   **Open as Web App** switched on.
4. **Tap Add.** Keep the name "Certa". The Certa icon appears on your Home Screen.
5. **Open Certa from the Home Screen and sign in.** You only sign in once; the app keeps you
   signed in. Allow notifications when asked. On iPhone, expiry reminders only arrive in the
   Home Screen app, not in a Safari tab.

## Android

Use **Chrome**. Samsung Internet and Edge work too.

1. **Open Certa in Chrome.** Go to `https://<your-certa-address>/install`.
2. **Tap Install.** Tap **Install Certa** at the top of the page if you see it. Otherwise tap
   **⋮** at the top right, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
   In Samsung Internet: **≡** → **Add page to** → **Home screen**.
3. **Confirm.** Tap **Install** (or **Add**). Certa appears on your Home Screen and in the app
   drawer.
4. **Open Certa and sign in.** Press and hold the icon for shortcuts to **Log a flight** and
   **Missions**.

## Computer: Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Linux

Use **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**. Firefox can't install web apps on a computer.

1. **Open Certa in Chrome or Edge.** Go to `https://<your-certa-address>/install`.
2. **Choose Install.** Click **Install Certa** at the top of the page if you see it, or click
   the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow).
   From the menu instead:
   - Chrome: **⋮** → **Cast, save and share** → **Install page as app**
   - Edge: **⋯** → **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**
3. **Confirm.** Click **Install**. Certa opens in its own window and is added to the Start
   menu, Launchpad or app launcher.
4. **Pin it.**
   - Windows: right-click Certa in the taskbar → **Pin to taskbar**.
   - Mac: right-click the dock icon → **Options** → **Keep in Dock**.

**Mac with Safari** (macOS Sonoma or later): **File** → **Add to Dock** → **Add**.

---

## Good to know

- **No signal?** Flights you log are saved on the device and sync when you're back in
  coverage.
- **Updates** arrive on their own the next time you open the app with a connection.
- **Removing it:** on a phone, press and hold the icon and choose **Remove App** (iPhone) or
  **Uninstall** (Android). On a computer, open Certa and choose **Uninstall** from its menu.
- **Signed in on the website already?** The installed app on iPhone keeps its own sign-in, so
  sign in once more inside the app.
